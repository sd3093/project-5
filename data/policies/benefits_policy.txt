EMPLOYEE BENEFITS GUIDE
Nexus Technologies Inc.
Effective Date: January 1, 2025
Last Updated: April 1, 2025

1. OVERVIEW

Nexus Technologies is committed to providing a comprehensive benefits package that supports the health, financial security, and professional growth of our employees and their families. This guide summarizes the benefits available to eligible employees.

2. ELIGIBILITY

Full-time employees working 30 or more hours per week are eligible for all benefits starting on the first day of the month following their hire date. Part-time employees working 20-29 hours per week are eligible for limited benefits (dental, vision, 401k, and EAP only).

3. HEALTH INSURANCE

3.1 Medical Plans
We offer three medical plan options through Anthem Blue Cross:

Bronze Plan (HDHP): $75/month employee-only, $250/month family. $3,000 individual deductible, $6,000 family deductible. Eligible for Health Savings Account (HSA). Company contributes $1,000 annually to employee HSA.

Silver Plan (PPO): $150/month employee-only, $450/month family. $1,500 individual deductible, $3,000 family deductible. $25 copay for primary care, $50 copay for specialists.

Gold Plan (PPO): $250/month employee-only, $650/month family. $500 individual deductible, $1,000 family deductible. $15 copay for primary care, $30 copay for specialists. Includes fertility treatment coverage.

All plans cover preventive care at 100% with no deductible. Prescription drug coverage is included in all plans with tiered copays: $10 generic, $35 preferred brand, $60 non-preferred brand.

3.2 Dental Insurance
Provided through Delta Dental. $20/month employee-only, $55/month family. Covers preventive care at 100%, basic procedures at 80%, and major procedures at 50%. Annual maximum benefit: $2,000 per person. Orthodontic coverage for dependents under 19: $2,500 lifetime maximum.

3.3 Vision Insurance
Provided through VSP. $10/month employee-only, $25/month family. Annual eye exam with $10 copay. $150 allowance for frames or contact lenses every 12 months.

4. RETIREMENT BENEFITS

4.1 401(k) Plan
Employees may contribute up to the IRS annual limit ($23,500 for 2025, $31,000 for employees 50 and older). The company matches 100% of the first 4% of salary contributed. Company match vests over 3 years: 33% after year 1, 66% after year 2, 100% after year 3.

Enrollment is automatic at 3% of salary unless the employee opts out. Employees may change their contribution rate at any time through Fidelity NetBenefits.

4.2 Employee Stock Purchase Plan (ESPP)
Employees may purchase company stock at a 15% discount through payroll deductions. Enrollment windows open in January and July. Maximum contribution is 10% of base salary per pay period.

5. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE

5.1 Basic Life Insurance
The company provides life insurance equal to 2x annual salary at no cost to the employee, up to a maximum of $500,000. Supplemental life insurance may be purchased at group rates.

5.2 Short-Term Disability
Covers 60% of base salary for up to 12 weeks following a 7-day elimination period. Company-paid at no cost to employees.

5.3 Long-Term Disability
Covers 60% of base salary after short-term disability is exhausted, continuing until age 65 or return to work. Company-paid at no cost to employees.

6. ADDITIONAL BENEFITS

6.1 Employee Assistance Program (EAP)
Free confidential counseling services for employees and household members. Up to 8 sessions per issue per year. Available 24/7 at 1-800-555-0199 or through the Lyra Health platform.

6.2 Wellness Stipend
$75 per month ($900 annually) for fitness-related expenses including gym memberships, fitness equipment, fitness classes, and sports league fees. Submit receipts through Expensify.

6.3 Commuter Benefits
Pre-tax commuter benefits up to $315/month for public transit and $315/month for parking through WageWorks.

6.4 Tuition Reimbursement
Up to $5,250 per year for job-related courses at accredited institutions. Must be pre-approved by manager and HR. Minimum grade of B required for reimbursement. Employees must remain with the company for 12 months after reimbursement or repay the amount.

6.5 Cell Phone Reimbursement
$50/month for employees who regularly use personal phones for business purposes. See Expense Policy for details.

7. OPEN ENROLLMENT

The annual open enrollment period runs from November 1 to November 30. Changes made during open enrollment take effect January 1. Outside of open enrollment, changes can only be made within 30 days of a qualifying life event (marriage, birth of child, loss of other coverage, etc.).

8. COBRA

Upon separation from the company, employees may continue their health, dental, and vision coverage for up to 18 months under COBRA at their own expense (full premium plus 2% administrative fee).
