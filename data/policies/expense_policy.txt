TRAVEL AND EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
Nexus Technologies Inc.
Effective Date: January 1, 2025
Last Updated: February 10, 2025

1. PURPOSE

This policy provides guidelines for business-related travel and expense reimbursement at Nexus Technologies Inc. All employees must follow these guidelines when incurring expenses on behalf of the company.

2. GENERAL PRINCIPLES

All business expenses must be reasonable, necessary, and directly related to company business. Employees are expected to exercise good judgment and spend company funds as if they were their own. Excessive or lavish expenses will not be reimbursed. All expenses require original receipts.

3. APPROVAL REQUIREMENTS

Expenses under $100 do not require pre-approval but must be submitted with receipts within 30 days. Expenses between $100 and $500 require manager approval before purchase. Expenses over $500 require both manager approval and Finance Department approval. All travel must be pre-approved by the employee's manager at least 5 business days in advance.

4. TRAVEL

4.1 Air Travel
Employees should book economy class for domestic flights and flights under 6 hours. Business class may be booked for international flights over 6 hours with manager approval. Employees are encouraged to book flights at least 14 days in advance to obtain the best rates.

Frequent flyer miles earned on business travel belong to the employee. However, flight selection should prioritize cost and convenience over loyalty program preferences.

4.2 Ground Transportation
Rental cars may be used when public transportation or ride-sharing is not practical. Mid-size vehicles or smaller should be selected unless transporting equipment. Employees must purchase the rental company's insurance coverage. Ride-sharing services (Uber, Lyft) are reimbursable for business travel.

Personal vehicle mileage is reimbursed at the current IRS rate ($0.67 per mile for 2025). Parking fees and tolls are reimbursable with receipts.

4.3 Lodging
Hotel expenses are reimbursed up to $200 per night for domestic travel and $300 per night for international travel. Employees should select hotels that are reasonably close to the business destination. Stays with friends or family in lieu of hotel earn a $50 per night stipend.

5. MEALS

5.1 Individual Meals During Travel
Breakfast: up to $20
Lunch: up to $25
Dinner: up to $50
Alcohol is not reimbursable during individual meals.

5.2 Client and Team Meals
Client entertainment meals are reimbursable up to $75 per person with prior manager approval. The expense report must include the names of all attendees, the business purpose, and the company or client represented. Team meals (celebrations, offsites) are reimbursable up to $40 per person with manager approval.

6. TECHNOLOGY AND EQUIPMENT

Pre-approved software subscriptions and tools are reimbursable. Personal phone bills are reimbursed up to $50 per month if the employee regularly uses their personal phone for business. The one-time home office stipend of $500 is processed through this policy (see Remote Work Policy for details).

7. PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT

Conference registration fees are reimbursable with manager approval. Each employee has an annual professional development budget of $1,500 for courses, certifications, books, and conference fees. Unused professional development funds do not roll over to the next year.

8. NON-REIMBURSABLE EXPENSES

The following expenses are not reimbursable: personal entertainment, gym memberships, clothing (unless safety equipment), fines or traffic violations, first-class air travel upgrades, minibar charges, personal travel extensions beyond the business trip, and spouse or partner travel expenses.

9. SUBMISSION PROCESS

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of incurring the expense using the Expensify platform. Late submissions (beyond 60 days) will not be reimbursed without VP-level approval. Required information for each expense: date, vendor, amount, business purpose, and receipt image.

Approved expense reports are processed within 10 business days and reimbursed via direct deposit on the next regular payroll cycle.

10. VIOLATIONS

Submitting fraudulent or inflated expense reports is grounds for immediate termination and may result in legal action. Repeated violations of this policy will result in progressive disciplinary action. The Finance team conducts random audits of expense reports quarterly.

For questions about this policy, contact expenses@nexustech.com.
